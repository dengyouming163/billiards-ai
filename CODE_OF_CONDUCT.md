# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a healthy community:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and derogatory comments.
- Public or private harassment, including unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or issue threads.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
and any public or private communication related to the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through the repository's private
security advisory form (Security tab → Report a vulnerability). Reports are visible only to the
maintainers and are handled confidentially.

Maintainers will review every report and respond within seven days. Depending on severity they may:

1. Ask the author to edit or remove the offending content.
2. Issue a warning with an explanation of the expected behaviour.
3. Temporarily or permanently restrict participation in the project.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith face the same consequences as any other
participant. Enforcement decisions are recorded internally so that repeat behaviour is visible.
